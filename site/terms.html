Terms and conditions

These are the terms shown in the Shuusshy installer. Installing or using Shuusshy means you accept them.

1. Local processing

Shuusshy processes your microphone on your computer only. It records nothing, uploads nothing and needs no account. Shuusshy's own network use is limited to the optional update check, which downloads release information from GitHub (you can turn it off in Settings). On Windows, the WebView2 component that draws the window may contact Microsoft services on its own; it receives no audio or settings from Shuusshy.

2. Two-year validity of each release

Every official Shuusshy release is valid for two years from its release date. The dates are shown in Settings → Application and Updates.

  • 30 days before the end you see a reminder, and 7 days before a warning.
  • When a release expires, noise cancellation and the virtual microphone stop until you install a newer release. Your settings are kept and nothing is uninstalled. You can still export your settings and open the download page.

Newer releases restart the two-year period. The check uses your computer's clock and works offline.

3. Shuusshy Pro

RNNoise noise cancellation is free. DeepFilterNet, voice isolation and the presets built on them need a Shuusshy Pro license key, checked offline on your computer.

4. Virtual microphone

Shuusshy uses a virtual audio cable that you install separately under its maker's own terms: VB-CABLE by VB-Audio Software on Windows, BlackHole by Existential Audio on macOS. Shuusshy does not install or change drivers. If you choose to rename the virtual microphone “Shuusshy Microphone” on Windows, Windows asks for permission first, and the uninstaller offers to undo it.
